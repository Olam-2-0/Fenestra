## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene clarity of early morning desk light. It merges tactile warmth—reminiscent of fine Japanese and Korean paper goods—with the ethereal precision of luminous frosted glass. Designed for focused students and collaborative teams, the interface evokes psychological safety, intentional pacing, and unhurried intellectual clarity.

The aesthetic balance is strictly calibrated:
- **80% Calm Productivity:** Generous canvas margins, restful negative space, non-demanding contrast ratios, and structured layout harmony that reduces cognitive friction.
- **15% Tactile Personality:** Warm textured paper tones, organic pill geometry, and delicate hairline separators reminiscent of physical stationery.
- **5% Futuristic Magic:** Soft atmospheric amber glows, gentle backdrop blurs, and luminous ambient cues indicating dynamic state transitions.

## Colors

The palette simulates sunlight filtering through sheer curtains onto a clean work table. Rather than stark digital whites or clinical cool grays, every hue is grounded in warmth.

- **Primary Background (`#F7F5EF`):** A serene ivory canvas that eliminates optical glare during prolonged study sessions.
- **Primary Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp morning white for floating panels and direct working sheets.
- **Subtle Surface (`#F0EDE4`):** Soft cream tint for nested containers, metadata bands, and inactive states.
- **Primary Accent (`#6750A4`):** Deep contemplative plum/violet for primary interactive cues, focused tabs, and progressive indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#B8E6D0`):** Soft sage mint for completion states, restorative badges, and mindful achievements.
- **Tertiary Accent / Luminescence (`#F4C95D`):** Warm amber glow for dynamic highlights, active timers, and contextual illumination.
- **Hairline Neutral (`#E8E5DC`):** Delicate outline tone to establish structure without visual clutter.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Deep charcoal (`#24232A`) guarantees sharp, low-strain legibility; warm slate (`#77747D`) supports secondary information.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy balances modern geometry with friendly humanist contours using Plus Jakarta Sans across all layers.

- **Headlines:** Use tighter negative letter-spacing and moderate weights (500–600) to maintain an intimate editorial tone, avoiding aggressive display weights.
- **Body:** Slightly enlarged tracking and relaxed vertical line heights (1.5–1.6) evoke the cadence of an open novel or clean journal page.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small tracking adjustments are restrained; keep labels mostly in title/sentence case to preserve warmth.

## Layout & Spacing

The canvas is treated as a clean physical desk with distinct study zones. 

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views adhere to a modular fluid 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, allowing central work panes to stay focused (max-width `1280px`). Tablet scales to an 8-column layout, and mobile collapses into a single-column sequence with 20px padding.
- **Rhythm & Empty Space:** Breathing room takes precedence over density. High-order content sections require generous margins (`2.5rem` on desktop) to foster an unhurried, focused mindset.
- **Component Proportions:** Interior card gaps follow consistent multiples of 4px/8px, prioritizing vertical comfort over dense horizontal cramming.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is inspired by frosted desk accessories and layered parchment rather than artificial drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: `#F7F5EF` canvas.
  - Tier 1: `#FFFFFF` panels layered with single-pixel hairline borders (`#E8E5DC`).
  - Tier 2 (Floating Glass): `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle interior edge highlight `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
- **Shadow Quality:** Shadows are ultra-diffused, tinted with warm plum/neutral tones: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(36, 35, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 35, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Atmospheric Glow:** Interactive highlights utilize an amber-plum light bleed: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(244, 201, 93, 0.35)`. Harsh black shadows and high-contrast bevels are never permitted.

## Shapes

The visual geometry employs soft, welcoming radii that resemble smooth river stones and rounded stationery sheets:

- Standard functional surfaces (cards, side sheets, modal dialogs) sit between 18px and 24px corner radius.
- Small interactive items (badges, tags, search bars, and utility buttons) utilize gentle capsule pill contours (`9999px`) to maintain tactile playfulness.
- Form controls (inputs, dropdowns) match a clean 12px–14px continuous curvature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep plum (`#6750A4`) with crisp white typography. Hover introduces an ambient amber light spread (`0 4px 16px rgba(103, 80, 164, 0.25)`). Corner radius: `9999px` (capsule).
- **Secondary:** Warm ivory card backing (`#F0EDE4`) with deep charcoal text (`#24232A`) and a subtle hairline border (`#E8E5DC`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent base, plum text, transitioning to `rgba(103, 80, 164, 0.06)` on hover with smooth spring easing.

### Cards & Panels
- **Study Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` background with an 18px–22px rounded radius, framed by a delicate border (`#E8E5DC`).
- **Ambient Focus Card:** Semi-translucent frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`) layered over the warm background with an internal white border reflection and gentle diffused shadow.

### Inputs & Search Bars
- Background set to pure `#FFFFFF` or subtle `#F0EDE4` with 12px roundedness.
- Hairline outline in `#E8E5DC`. Focus states transition to a plum border ring (`#6750A4`) paired with an ethereal 3px warm amber halo blur (`rgba(244, 201, 93, 0.3)`).

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) tags using `#F0EDE4` or muted sage (`rgba(184, 230, 208, 0.25)`) with deep charcoal or forest plum text.
- Selected filter state activates a subtle glowing sage or plum halo border.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes: 20px rounded squares (`6px` radius), bordered in `#E8E5DC`. On check, morphs into `#6750A4` with a crisp white tick mark and momentary micro-glow.
- Radios: Circular nested rings with an inner plum pip and soft mint ambient ping animation on selection.

### Ambient Study Timer / Progress Indicators
- Circular, pill-based track elements rendered in delicate hairline cream tracks, filled with plum and illuminated at the leading edge by an amber tip.
